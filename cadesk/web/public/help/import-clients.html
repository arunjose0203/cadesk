Import clients from Excel

Bring your whole client list in at once from an .xlsx or .csv file.

  1. Open Clients and choose Import from Excel.
  2. Download the template. It has one column per field, including the tax registrations (PAN, GSTIN, TAN) and any custom fields your firm has added.
  3. Fill one row per client. Only the name is required; leave the code empty and CaDesk numbers the client for you. Groups that don’t exist yet are created.
  4. Upload the file. CaDesk checks every row the same way the client form does, and shows what it would do without saving anything: rows that are fine, rows with errors and why, and columns it ignored.
  5. Fix the errors in your file and upload again, or choose to skip the rows with errors. Then confirm the import.

Your plan sets how many clients the firm can have (Starter: 100, Growth: 500, Enterprise: no limit). If the file would take you past the limit, nothing is imported and CaDesk tells you how many more you can add.
