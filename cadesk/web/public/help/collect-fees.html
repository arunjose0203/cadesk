Collect fees online

Send clients a payment link for an invoice; it is marked paid when they pay.

  1. Your CaDesk administrator connects the firm’s Razorpay account once. Until then the button below doesn’t appear.
  2. In Billing & Time, find an invoice that has been sent and has a balance due, and choose Payment link.
  3. Copy the link, send it on WhatsApp, or open it to check. The link is for the balance due at that moment.
  4. When the client pays by UPI, card or net banking, Razorpay tells CaDesk and the payment is recorded against the invoice. A part payment marks the invoice part-paid.

Payments reach your firm’s Razorpay account directly; CaDesk never holds the money.
