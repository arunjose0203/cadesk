Plans, desks and users

What each plan includes, and what happens at a limit.

PlanUsersClientsDesks
Starter3100Practice (tasks, deadlines, clients, billing, client portal), trial balance, fixed assets, income tax, advance tax, TDS, notices, tax audit (3CD), GSTR-1 and the 3B check, MCA, KYC, DSC, payroll
Growth15500Everything in Starter, plus the audit workspace, CARO, Schedule III, branch audit, audit committee, internal audit, AQMM, GST reconciliation, GSTR-9, e-invoicing, bank feeds, ledger sync, litigation, related parties, 15CA/15CB and practice health
EnterpriseNo limitNo limitEverything in Growth, plus transfer pricing, cost audit, secretarial audit, anti-profiteering, ESG/BRSR, IBC and UAE corporate tax

Desks outside your plan show the plan’s name in the sidebar. Opening one explains which plan it needs; partners who manage settings can go to Plan & Billing from there to upgrade. Upgrading doesn’t change any data.

Some desks (bank feeds, e-invoicing, UAE tax and a few others) also have to be switched on for the firm in settings.

The user limit counts active users plus invitations that haven’t been accepted yet. Cancel an old invitation to free its place.
