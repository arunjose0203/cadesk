Security and privacy

How CaDesk protects your firm’s and your clients’ data.

  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app; partners can require it for everyone.
  • Roles decide which desks and actions each person has. Changes reach people within 15 minutes.
  • Automatic sign-out after the period of inactivity set for the firm.
  • Each firm’s data is kept apart by the database itself, not only by the app.
  • Audit log: partners can see who did what and when.
  • Data stays in India.

Your firm and the DPDP Act

For your clients’ personal data, your firm is the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and CaDesk processes the data for you, as set out in the Data Processing Terms. If a client asks to see, correct or erase their data, you can find, export, correct and delete their records in CaDesk.
