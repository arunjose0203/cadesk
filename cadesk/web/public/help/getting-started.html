Getting started

Set up your firm in CaDesk in about half an hour.

  1. Create the firm. On the sign-in page choose Create a firm, enter the firm name, your name, work email and a password, and accept the terms. You become the firm’s first partner.
  2. Turn on two-step sign-in. Open My Account (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and scan the code with an authenticator app. Ask everyone in the firm to do the same.
  3. Bring in your clients. Add them one by one in Clients, or import a spreadsheet: see Import clients from Excel.
  4. Invite your team. In Team, invite each person by email and pick a role. A role decides which desks and actions they see.
  5. Check the deadlines. Deadlines lists GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates for each client from the registrations on the client record. Assign each one to a person.

Finding your way

  • Today shows what is overdue, what is due this week and your tasks.
  • Press Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac) to jump to any desk by typing its name.
  • Pin the desks you use most with the pin that appears when you point at a desk in the sidebar.
  • Each desk has its own web address, so you can bookmark it or send the link to a colleague.
  • The button at the bottom of the sidebar switches between light and dark.
