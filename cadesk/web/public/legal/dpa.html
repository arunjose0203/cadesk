1. Roles

These terms form part of the Terms of Service. For personal data of the firm's clients and of other people the firm records in CaDesk ("client data"), the firm is the data fiduciary and [operator's legal name] ("we") is the data processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

2. What we do with client data

  • We process client data only to provide CaDesk and only on the firm's instructions, which are given by using the app and its settings.
  • We do not sell it, use it for our own purposes, or use it to train AI models.
  • Everyone at [operator] who can reach it is bound by confidentiality.

3. Security safeguards

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Each firm's data separated in the database by row-level security.
  • Role-based access, two-step sign-in, automatic sign-out.
  • Virus scanning and content checks on uploaded files.
  • Logs of access kept for at least one year, and daily backups.

4. Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a breach affecting client data we will tell the firm without delay, with what we know of its nature, extent, likely impact and the steps taken, so that the firm can inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected within the times the DPDP Rules set.

5. Requests from data principals

The firm answers requests from its clients to access, correct or erase their data; CaDesk lets the firm find, export, correct and delete records. If a request reaches us we pass it to the firm.

8. At the end

When the agreement ends the firm can export its data for [30] days. We then delete client data, including from backups within [90] days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

9. Professional confidentiality

We understand that client data is held by the firm under the confidentiality duties of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, and we treat it accordingly.
