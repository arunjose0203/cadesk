Who we are

CaDesk is practice software for chartered accountant firms in India, run by [operator's legal name], [registered address] ("we"). This notice explains how we handle personal data about the people who use CaDesk: partners and staff of a firm, and the firm's clients when they use the client portal.

A firm decides why and how its clients' data is processed, so for that data the firm is the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and we process it for the firm. Our Data Processing Terms cover that. If you are a firm's client, ask the firm about how it uses your data; you can also write to us and we will pass your request on.

We process this data because you or your firm gave consent when accepting this notice, or because it is needed to provide the service your firm asked for. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use your firm's data to train AI models.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run CaDesk, under contracts that bind them to protect it:

  • Hosting and storage: [provider], data centres in India.
  • Email and SMS delivery: [provider].
  • Online payments, when your firm turns them on: Razorpay Software Private Limited.

We disclose data to authorities only where Indian law requires it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Data is stored in India. We keep account data while your firm's subscription is active. When a firm leaves, we keep its data for [30] days so it can be exported, then delete it, except security logs, which we keep for one year as the DPDP Rules, 2025 require, and records the law requires us to keep longer (such as tax invoices).

How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, separation of each firm's data in the database, two-step sign-in, role-based access, virus scanning of uploaded files, backups, and logs of access. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent (withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use CaDesk);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

Write to our Grievance Officer: [name], [email], [address]. We reply within [30] days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

CaDesk is for professionals. Accounts are not meant for anyone under 18.
